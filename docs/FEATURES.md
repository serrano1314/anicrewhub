# AnimeCrew Hub — Feature Guide & Product Overview

> **Welcome to AnimeCrew Hub!**  
> *(Formerly known as Anime Tracker)*  
> AnimeCrew Hub is your dedicated anime collection hub and community platform. While it quietly and seamlessly keeps track of your viewing progress in the background, its core purpose is celebrating your passion for anime: building your personal collection, discovering trending shows, connecting with fellow fans, and sharing where to watch.

---

## 🌟 What is AnimeCrew Hub?

Anime lovers often watch across multiple platforms—Netflix, Crunchyroll, and independent streaming sites. When episodes are split or missing between services, you lose track of what you've watched, what you loved, and where to find the rest.

**AnimeCrew Hub** brings everything together into one unified, elegant space. It is designed to be your permanent digital anime home:
- **Your Collection First**: Organize, rate, and showcase your entire anime journey.
- **A Connected Community**: Follow friends, explore each other's collections, and share watch sites.
- **Invisible Tracking**: With an optional companion Chrome extension, your watch progress syncs automatically without interrupting your viewing.

---

## 🚀 Current Features (What You Can See & Do)

### 1. Account & Personal Profile
- **Quick Registration & Secure Login**: Fast, secure email-and-password authentication with persistent login sessions.
- **Customizable Profiles**: Personalize your profile with custom preset anime avatars and display your public anime statistics (episodes watched, collection count).
- **Terms & Privacy Transparency**: Clear terms of service and dedicated privacy controls to keep you in control of your account.

### 2. Spotlight & Anime Discovery
- **Trending & New Anime Carousel**: An interactive spotlight carousel on your home page showcasing the hottest current releases, upcoming titles, and seasonal favorites.
- **Live AniList Search**: Search through thousands of anime series, movies, OVAs, and specials in real-time.
- **Rich Anime Details**:
  - Full synopses and descriptions.
  - Formats (TV, Movie, OVA, ONA, Special), airing seasons, years, and episode totals.
  - Official streaming links (Crunchyroll, Netflix, etc.) sourced directly from AniList.
  - Community-shared and personal watch links.

### 3. Your Personal Anime Collection
- **5 Intuitive Status Shelves**:
  - `Watching` — Series you are actively following.
  - `Completed` — Shows you have finished.
  - `Plan to Watch` — Your anime watchlist for the future.
  - `On Hold` — Series you've paused and plan to pick back up.
  - `Dropped` — Anime you decided not to continue.
- **Favorites & Star Ratings**:
  - Pin your all-time favorites to give them pride of place on your profile.
  - Rate shows on a 1–10 star scale to reflect your personal taste.
- **Flexible Episode Progress**:
  - **Single-Click Progress**: Mark individual episodes as watched or increment with a quick `+1`.
  - **Bulk Catch-Up (`Mark Up To Episode N`)**: Watching a 1,000+ episode epic like *One Piece*? Jump straight to episode 811 in one single click without clicking hundreds of times.
  - **Filler & Skip Support**: Skipped recap or filler episodes? Progress tracks actual watched episodes rather than just the highest number.
  - **Movies & OVAs**: Movies and single-release OVAs naturally count as 1 episode.
  - **Unknown Episode Lengths**: Seamlessly handles long-running shows whose total episode count is not yet known.

### 4. Watch Sites & Direct Links
- **Personal Watch Sites**: Save the exact websites and custom URL templates you use to watch your anime.
- **Smart Watch-Link Hierarchy**: When you click an episode, AnimeCrew Hub presents watch links in your preferred order:
  1. Your saved custom sites for that anime.
  2. Official streaming partners (Crunchyroll, Netflix, etc.).
  3. A quick fallback Google search link.

### 5. Social & Community Hub
- **Search Users**: Find friends, reviewers, and anime enthusiasts by username through authenticated user search.
- **Follow & Unfollow System**:
  - Follow public profiles instantly with a single click.
  - For private profiles, send a follow request that the user can approve or decline.
  - View follower and following counts.
- **Browse Shared Collections**:
  - Visit followed creators and friends to view their public collections, watch statuses, and ratings.
  - Discover what shows other people in the community are currently enjoying.
- **Share & Copy Reusable Watch Sites**:
  - See the watch sites that other community members use.
  - Safely copy useful site templates to your own account with full source attribution.
- **In-App Notification Center**:
  - Interactive notification bell in the navigation bar.
  - Instant alerts for new followers, pending follow requests, and accepted requests.

### 6. Granular Privacy Controls
You decide how social you want to be:
- **Profile Visibility**: Toggle between **Public** (discoverable in user search) and **Private** (requires approval before anyone can follow).
- **Collection Sharing**: Independently enable or disable public viewing of your anime collection.
- **Watch Site Sharing**: Choose whether other members can see and copy your reusable watch sites.

### 7. Companion Chrome Extension
- **Automated Viewing Sync**: While you watch anime on supported streaming websites, the extension detects the anime title and episode number. Passing 85% of the video automatically updates your progress in your AnimeCrew Hub collection.
- **Manual Fallback**: If a video player runs inside an isolated iframe, use the extension popup to click **"Mark current episode"** or **"+1"** with zero friction.
- **Secure Token Authentication**: Operates via dedicated, hashed extension tokens generated in your Settings—keeping your main web account credentials completely safe and private.

### 8. Data Freedom & Portability
- **JSON Backup & Restore**: Download your entire collection, episode progress, ratings, and watch sites at any time.
- **Safe Import**: Easily restore or merge your backup JSON into your account without losing existing data.

## 🔮 What's Coming in the Future (Roadmap)

AnimeCrew Hub is expanding from a personal collection and tracking tool into a vibrant, full-featured anime community. Here is what you can look forward to in upcoming releases:

1. **Reviews & Ratings Hub**:
   - Write comprehensive reviews and share in-depth thoughts on your favorite (or least favorite) anime.
   - Community-aggregated ratings and community score breakdowns.
2. **Comments & Episode Discussions**:
   - Episode-by-episode comment sections and threaded discussions to chat about plot twists, animations, and theories.
   - Spoiler tags and moderation tools to keep discussions safe and friendly.
3. **Community Activity Feed**:
   - A real-time timeline showing what your followed friends are currently watching, rating, or adding to their watchlists.
4. **Custom Lists & Tags**:
   - Create and share curated lists (e.g., *"Top 90s Cyberpunk Classics"*, *"Best Cozy Slice-of-Life"*, *"Guilty Pleasures"*).
5. **Airing Reminders & Release Calendar**:
   - Personalized release schedules notifying you the moment a new episode of a show on your `Watching` list airs.
6. **Broader Extension Support**:
   - Expanded out-of-the-box support for popular anime streaming platforms and enhanced player detection.

## 💡 Quick Summary for New Members

| Need | Where to Go in AnimeCrew Hub |
|---|---|
| **Discover what's hot** | Home / Search page carousel and instant AniList search |
| **Organize my anime** | **My Collection** (Watching, Completed, Plan to Watch, etc.) |
| **Track a long anime quickly** | Detail page → **"I watched up to episode..."** |
| **Find friends** | **Search Users** → Follow profiles |
| **See where others watch** | User profile → Shared Reusable Watch Sites → Copy to your sites |
| **Track automatically while streaming** | **Settings → Extension Tokens** → Chrome Extension popup |
| **Backup my list** | **Settings → Backup & Restore** → Export JSON |
